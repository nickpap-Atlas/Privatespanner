The founder

Marcus & Private Spanner

I'm Marcus, son of Peter Papanicolaou, founder and owner of Spartan Motor Company. I grew up in the garage where our family has spent nearly two decades developing race cars, so cars have been part of my life for as long as I can remember.

Since then I've worked hands-on in prestige, performance and classic workshops across Sydney, and noticed that owners of great cars often end up managing them instead of enjoying them.

Private Spanner is my answer to that. I'll be the one person who knows your cars, knows the right specialists, and makes sure everything is done properly. You get to drive them.
